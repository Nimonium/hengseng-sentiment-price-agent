📋 Methodology, Out-of-Sample Validation & Honest Scientific Limitations

This autonomous pipeline implements an institutional-grade, zero-leakage workflow combining social listening, neural news search, and intraday equity price action across HKEX and KRX.

  • 1. Truncated 10-Day Window: Evaluated strictly on the last 10 calendar days comprising only 5 distinct trading sessions (35 hourly bars for HKEX, 30 hourly bars for KRX). Pre-window bars are used exclusively for indicator warm-up.
  • 2. Small Out-of-Sample Test Sizes: Per-ticker walk-forward evaluation yields small out-of-sample test samples (N = 24 for HKEX tickers, N = 20 for KRX tickers; N = 136 and N = 116 pooled). While cross-sectional pooling increases sample density, statistical power remains bounded by short calendar duration.
  • 3. Thin Social UGC Coverage: HKEX has only one thin user-generated source (LIHKG, 4 posts in window) because Futu/Moomoo and ETNet have zero actors on the Apify Store, and tested Telegram channels failed (login wall / preview disabled). KRX features dense retail discussion via Naver 종목토론실 (400 posts in window). TeamBlind runs succeeded on Apify (60 items/ticker) but datasets were locked by Apify's monthly usage cap.
  • 4. Zero-Lookahead Alignment & Decay: Point-in-time causal join ensures zero leakage. Overnight and weekend posts roll into the market open bar, with exponential decay (6h half-life) applied across subsequent inactive bars.
  • 5. Superiority of Naive Benchmarks: In low-signal intraday regimes, the Naive Zero-Return benchmark strictly minimizes MAE over RidgeCV regression across all assets and pooled models. Directional accuracy shows no statistically significant alpha against 50% or naive baselines (binomial p ≥ 0.05 across all tests).
  • 6. Free-Tier Scraping Limits & Reproducibility: Apify's $5.00 monthly usage limit halts live scraping once depleted. Full zero-token reviewer reproducibility is guaranteed via --use-sample loading from the committed data/sample/ bundle.
